The Review tab scans the whole demo for moments where a player's crosshair stays on an enemy who is behind a wall while that enemy moves, and for very fast snaps onto a target right before a shot. They are leads to watch, not proof: check each one in Eyes view with X-ray on.

Hitboxes are posed the way the server poses them for a shot, which is not the model you see: in TFC every class gets the scout's hitboxes, the legs don't run, and the body faces where the player looks. A bullet is traced along the centre of the aim (shotgun spread isn't), against where the server had everyone at that moment. The server's lag compensation moves targets back by the shooter's ping when it checks a hit, so the boxes are not exactly what the shooter saw.
